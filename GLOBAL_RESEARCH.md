## Purpose

This register supplies a shared evidence base for the frozen building-glass cleaning, façade-interface, work-at-height, architectural-film, procurement, and handover catalog. It records what public primary or authoritative sources establish, what they do not establish, and which project facts or licensed texts must be obtained before consequential claims are published.

The register does not certify a person, access system, chemical, glass construction, film product, installed system, façade, test result, warranty, price, or legal interpretation. A material property is not automatically an installed-system result, and a test method is not evidence that an unnamed product or project passed it.

### KR-02 — Indonesian building governance and maintenance baseline

- **Sources:** [PP No. 16 Tahun 2021 — BPK](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/161846/pp-no-16-tahun-2021), [Permen PU No. 24/PRT/M/2008 — BPK](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/104476/permen-), [official Permen PU 24/2008 PDF](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/95204/PermenPU24-2008.pdf)
- **Grade:** A.
- **Purpose:** Anchor surveys, building operation, preventive maintenance, records, and escalation in Indonesian building-governance context.
- **Summary:** Indonesian building regulation treats building reliability and operation as managed responsibilities. The official maintenance guideline supplies a public maintenance/perawatan framework that includes cleaning, inspection, testing, repair or replacement, programming, equipment, and competent personnel.
- **Grounded facts:** PP 16/2021 is the implementing regulation for Indonesia's building law. Permen PU 24/2008 is an official guideline for maintenance and care of buildings; its text distinguishes routine maintenance from restorative care and supports planned inspection, cleaning, records, equipment, and personnel rather than appearance-only cleaning.
- **Incorporation:** Use this baseline to explain why zone inventories, recurring logs, condition escalation, and coordination with building management matter.
- **Limits/recheck:** Do not turn a national framework into a project permit list, maintenance frequency, inspection interval, or technical acceptance value. Confirm local government, building-function, ownership, and contract requirements under GATE-02.

- **Summary:** Permenaker 9/2016 remains the named Indonesian K3 regulation for work at height, but as of 3 July 2026 Permenaker 11/2026 partially revoked Article 1 point 14 and Article 29(3). Any legal summary must read the current texts together.
- **Grounded facts:** The BPK status record marks Permenaker 9/2016 as in force with a partial revocation. The 2026 instrument governs labor-inspection procedures and makes the listed partial changes; it does not erase the rest of the work-at-height regulation.
- **Incorporation:** State that elevated glass work requires project-specific hazard control, competent roles, inspected equipment, a workable emergency/rescue arrangement, and verification against the current consolidated legal position.
- **Limits/recheck:** Do not reproduce qualification categories, certificate rules, equipment numbers, anchor loads, weather thresholds, or rescue techniques from memory. Resolve GATE-01, GATE-02, and GATE-03 before precise claims.

- **Grade:** A.

### KR-06 — Contamination, damage, and accessible-surface diagnosis

- **Sources:** [NGA Proper Procedures for Cleaning Architectural Glass Products (2023)](https://www.glass.org/sites/default/files/2023-12/FB01-00_2023_Proper_Procedures_Cleaning_Architectural_Glass_Products.pdf), [NGA Heat-Treated Glass Surfaces Are Different (2023)](https://www.glass.org/sites/default/files/2023-10/FB02-02_2023_Heat-Treated_Glass_Surfaces_Different.pdf), [SNI ISO 11479-1:2014 physical defects — BSN](https://pesta.bsn.go.id/produk/detail/10042-sniiso11479-12014), [SNI ISO 20492-2:2014 fogging test — BSN](https://pesta.bsn.go.id/produk/detail/20492220142020-sniiso20492-2%3A2014konfirmasi2020)
- **Grade:** A for BSN identity; C for industry guidance.
- **Purpose:** Separate removable contamination from scratches, etching, coating damage, edge/seal problems, or fogging inside a unit.
- **Summary:** Cleaning should begin with inspection and identification because deposits, construction debris, surface damage, and inaccessible internal fogging lead to different actions. Heat-treated surfaces can present cleaning risks not resolved by assuming all glass is equally tolerant.
- **Grounded facts:** A symptom's location matters: material on an accessible face may be test-cleaned, while moisture or fogging between sealed layers is not accessible-surface soil. Scratches, etching, coating loss, seal failure, and glass breakage are not cured by progressively stronger cleaning.
- **Incorporation:** Use observation fields for face/location, pattern, lighting, wet/dry response, adjacent moisture source, prior treatment, and photos; escalate irreversible or concealed conditions.
- **Limits/recheck:** Do not identify mold species, health risk, coating chemistry, sealed-unit failure cause, or restoration feasibility from a photograph. Exact defect or fogging criteria require GATE-01; consequential diagnosis requires GATE-04 and GATE-10.

### KR-07 — Cleaning sequence, tools, water, and material compatibility

- **Sources:** [NGA Proper Procedures for Cleaning Architectural Glass Products (2023)](https://www.glass.org/sites/default/files/2023-12/FB01-00_2023_Proper_Procedures_Cleaning_Architectural_Glass_Products.pdf), [NGA Heat-Treated Glass Surfaces Are Different (2023)](https://www.glass.org/sites/default/files/2023-10/FB02-02_2023_Heat-Treated_Glass_Surfaces_Different.pdf), [NGA/GANA Glazing Manual, 2022 IYOG edition](https://www.glass.org/sites/default/files/2023-01/GANA_Glazing_Manual_2022_pw.pdf)
- **Grade:** C.
- **Purpose:** Ground test-patch, least-aggressive-method, rinse, runoff, and inspection advice.
- **Summary:** Authoritative industry guidance supports identifying the glass and contamination, following glass/product manufacturer instructions, using clean compatible tools, controlling abrasive debris, testing a small area, rinsing, and inspecting. Cleaner pH alone cannot establish compatibility with coatings, film, sealants, frames, stone, or metal.
- **Grounded facts:** Scrapers, pads, abrasives, chemicals, pressure, water quality, dirty tools, and trapped particles can change risk. Construction debris and heat-treated glass need special caution. Runoff and overspray can affect adjacent façade materials.
- **Incorporation:** Describe a controlled sequence—survey, manufacturer/SDS/TDS check, protection, test patch, least-aggressive method, rinse, controlled drying, inspection, and escalation—without prescribing a universal recipe.
- **Limits/recheck:** Do not publish universal dilution, dwell time, pH range, scraper permission, pressure, water-purity number, or chemical compatibility. Obtain the exact product TDS/SDS and substrate instructions under GATE-05.

### KR-08 — Façade seals, frames, drainage, and water investigation

- **Sources:** [Dow Asia Structural Sealant Glazing Manual](https://www.dow.com/documents/63/63-6132-01-structural-sealant-glazing-manual-asia.pdf), [ASTM E331-00(2023) official scope](https://store.astm.org/standards/e331), [ASTM E2190-19 official scope](https://store.astm.org/standards/e2190), [ASTM E06.51 current window/façade standards list](https://www.astm.org/membership-participation/technical-committees/committee-e06/subcommittee-e06/jurisdiction-e0651)
- **Grade:** B for test-method scope; D for the named sealant manufacturer's manual.
- **Purpose:** Prevent cleaning or film work from being confused with seal, drainage, façade, or insulating-glass diagnosis.
- **Summary:** Glass is part of an envelope assembly with frames, gaskets, sealants, joints, weeps, drains, coatings, and edge seals. Water-test methods and durability standards apply to defined specimens/configurations; a test-method citation is not proof of a leak cause or installed-system pass.
- **Grounded facts:** Structural/weatherseal silicone work requires substrate, adhesion, joint-design, cleaning, compatibility, quality-control, and project records specific to the system. ASTM E331 addresses water penetration under controlled pressure difference. ASTM E2190 addresses insulating-glass unit durability.
- **Incorporation:** Record pre-existing cracks, brittle or detached seals, blocked drainage, corrosion, open joints, edge damage, and water timing before work; distinguish cleaning runoff from a reproducible envelope leak.
- **Limits/recheck:** Never advise field sealant repair, joint dimensions, adhesion acceptance, leak attribution, pressure, or pass/fail criteria from these abstracts. Resolve GATE-01, GATE-04, and GATE-10.

### KR-09 — Risk-based preventive maintenance and records

- **Sources:** [Permen PU No. 24/PRT/M/2008 — BPK](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/104476/permen-), [official Permen PU 24/2008 PDF](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/95204/PermenPU24-2008.pdf), [NGA/GANA Glazing Manual, 2022 IYOG edition](https://www.glass.org/sites/default/files/2023-01/GANA_Glazing_Manual_2022_pw.pdf)
- **Grade:** A for Indonesian guidance; C for glazing-industry guidance.
- **Purpose:** Ground cleaning frequency, inspection logs, vendor KPIs, and escalation as a managed program.
- **Summary:** Maintenance is a recurring management process, not a universal calendar interval. Exposure, orientation, pollution, coast/traffic conditions, façade geometry, occupancy, access, prior results, safety constraints, and manufacturer instructions should change inspection and service planning.
- **Grounded facts:** Useful logs identify zones, dates, observations, products/methods, weather/site conditions, access used, exceptions, damage, photos, and follow-up. An appearance KPI alone can hide safety, damage, residue, access, and documentation failures.
- **Incorporation:** Teach a risk-based review cycle: baseline, inspect, act, verify, record, trend, and escalate. Coordinate cleaning mobilization with authorized inspection only where scopes and competent roles remain distinct.
- **Limits/recheck:** Do not state a universal cleaning frequency or inspection interval. Project-specific schedules require exposure history, contract, manufacturer, building-management, and legal inputs under GATE-02 and GATE-09.

- **Limits/recheck:** IWFA is industry guidance and NFRC is a US scheme, not Indonesian approval. Do not promise night privacy, comfort, energy savings, glass retention, forced-entry resistance, or compliance from a category name.

### KR-12 — Safety/security film is a tested-system question

- **Sources:** [IWFA Safety & Security Education Guide 2026](https://iwfa.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Safety-Security-Education-Guide-2026.pdf), [IWFA explanation of ASTM F3561](https://iwfa.com/articles/astm-f3561-22-an-explanation/), [ASTM F1642/F1642M-25 official scope](https://store.astm.org/f1642_f1642m-25.html), [3M Safety & Security Window Film Installation Guidelines, January 2025](https://multimedia.3m.com/mws/media/1704892O/security-window-film.pdf)
- **Grade:** B for ASTM scope; C for industry guidance; D for named-product installation guidance.
- **Purpose:** Prevent film-only claims about impact, forced entry, blast, burglary, ballistics, or life safety.
- **Summary:** Safety/security outcomes belong to a complete tested glazing configuration, potentially including glass, film, attachment, frame, anchors, dimensions, installation, and support conditions. Film by itself cannot inherit the result of a different assembly.
- **Grounded facts:** IWFA's 2026 guide calls for threat-specific professional design and cautions against exaggerated claims. Its F3561 explanation states that film alone does not meet a full-system forced-entry test. ASTM F1642/F1642M-25 is a test method for glazing/glazing systems/retrofit systems under airblast; a hazard rating belongs to the tested configuration. IWFA states it knows of no window films with ballistic properties.
- **Incorporation:** Ask for the threat statement, complete configuration, current laboratory report, standard edition, specimen dimensions, attachment details, result/classification, limitations, installer authorization, and project design review.
- **Limits/recheck:** These sources do not establish Indonesian code acceptance or suitability for a specific threat. Never say “bulletproof,” “blast-proof,” “anti-burglary,” or “compliant” without GATE-07 and GATE-08.

- **Limits/recheck:** Do not state that a symptom is normal, promise a warranty, assign cause/liability, or prescribe universal removal/cleaning. Resolve GATE-05, GATE-06, and GATE-09.

### KR-16 — QC, traceability, acceptance, and handover

- **Sources:** [NGA/GANA Glazing Manual, 2022 IYOG edition](https://www.glass.org/sites/default/files/2023-01/GANA_Glazing_Manual_2022_pw.pdf), [IWFA inspection-guideline overview](https://iwfa.com/inspection-guidelines/), [Permen PU No. 24/PRT/M/2008 — BPK](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/104476/permen-)
- **Grade:** A/C.
- **Purpose:** Define a defensible evidence chain from baseline through acceptance and operation.
- **Summary:** Acceptance should compare completed work with the agreed scope, baseline, product/system instructions, viewing or test conditions, cure status, exceptions, and approved criteria. Handover connects a one-time project to future maintenance and warranty administration.
- **Grounded facts:** Useful records include zones, product/manufacturer, batch or traceability identifier where available, glass/configuration, dates, installer/vendor, access method, site conditions, photos, measurements where agreed, damage/exclusions, care instructions, warranty issuer, notice route, and open items.
- **Incorporation:** Use separate checklists for cleaning and film, with signed exceptions and deferred areas; issue as-built/area records, care rules, allowed products, inspection timing, contacts, and warranty documents.
- **Limits/recheck:** Do not invent visual tolerances, measurement protocols, cure periods, remedies, or warranty coverage. Apply GATE-01, GATE-06, and GATE-09 to the actual contract and product.

### KR-17 — Procurement, comparable scope, prices, and change control

- **Sources:** [`ARTICLE_CATALOG.md`](ARTICLE_CATALOG.md), [`TOPICAL_AUTHORITY.md`](TOPICAL_AUTHORITY.md), [Permen PU No. 24/PRT/M/2008 — BPK](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/104476/permen-)
- **Grade:** A for repository scope and Indonesian maintenance context; G for live commercial facts.
- **Purpose:** Support RFQs, scope normalization, cost-driver explanations, vendor comparison, and hidden-condition governance.
- **Summary:** A useful quotation must identify zones/quantities, observed condition, intended outcome, access/site constraints, operating hours, protection, method assumptions, product/configuration, evidence, QC, exclusions, schedule, warranty, and change process. Price follows the defined scope and date.
- **Grounded facts:** Cleaning cost can change with area, contamination, access, frequency, hours, protection, water/logistics, and mobilization. Film cost can change with exact product, glazing compatibility, area/layout, waste, access, existing-film removal, attachment system, evidence, and warranty.
- **Incorporation:** Teach buyers to normalize inclusions, exclusions, quantities, methods, proof, schedule, QC, and risk allocation before comparing totals; record pre-existing and hidden conditions with an approval path.
- **Limits/recheck:** No public source here establishes a current project price, contractor capability, insurance, availability, tax, contract right, or change-order entitlement. Obtain dated quotations and project/legal review under GATE-09.

### KR-18 — Standards, structural context, documents, and claim verification

- **Sources:** [SNI 1727:2020 — BSN](https://pesta.bsn.go.id/produk/detail/12927-sni17272020), [ASTM E1300-24 official scope](https://store.astm.org/e1300-24.html), [SNI 6389:2020 — BSN](https://pesta.bsn.go.id/produk/detail/13242-sni63892020), [Permenaker No. 9 Tahun 2016 — Kemnaker JDIH](https://jdih.kemnaker.go.id/peraturan/detail/1210/peraturan-menteri-nomor-9-tahun-2016)
- **Grade:** A/B.
- **Purpose:** Teach document roles and stop standards names, logos, or test-method citations from becoming unsupported compliance claims.
- **Summary:** SNI 1727:2020 is the active Indonesian standard for minimum design loads and related criteria; ASTM E1300 is a practice for determining load resistance of glass in buildings. Neither source makes a remote cleaning/film article a structural assessment. TDS, SDS, method statements, risk assessments, test reports, certificates, insurance records, permits, and warranties answer different questions.
- **Grounded facts:** A standards catalog establishes document identity/status; a full standard controls its technical requirements. A certificate must be checked for issuer, holder, scope, status, date, and relevance. A test report must match the claimed material or system configuration. A logo or “ISO/K3/rope access” phrase is not sufficient evidence.
- **Incorporation:** Explain each document's function and give a verification workflow: source/issuer, exact identifier, edition/date, subject, scope, configuration, result, status, exclusions, and project relevance.
- **Limits/recheck:** Do not quote structural loads, glass capacity, safety factors, acceptance criteria, certificate validity, or permit conclusions without GATE-01, GATE-02, GATE-03, and GATE-07.

## Cross-catalog fact bank

- A glass face, coating, laminate, heat treatment, cavity, frame, gasket, sealant, edge seal, film, and attachment system are distinct components; one component's data does not automatically describe the assembly.
- Appearance can support triage but cannot reliably identify concealed glass construction, coating surface, edge quality, seal failure cause, or structural capacity.
- A test method defines how a specimen/configuration is assessed. Passing evidence requires the actual current report and a match between the tested and proposed systems.
- Center-of-glazing product values do not prove whole-window, room, HVAC, comfort, or building-energy outcomes.
- Safety/security claims are threat- and configuration-specific; film alone must not inherit a complete-system result.
- Cleaning compatibility is not established by pH alone. Exact substrate, contamination, chemical, tool, water, adjacent finish, and manufacturer instructions matter.
- Access-method selection and safe operation are site decisions requiring competent people, current equipment/system evidence, public protection, weather controls, and rescue.
- Applied-film appearance changes during cure; acceptance must use the actual product instructions, contract, timing, viewing conditions, and agreed criteria.
- Maintenance intervals and prices are project facts, not universal editorial facts.
- Foreign standards, NFRC certification, NGA/IWFA/IRATA guidance, and manufacturer bulletins are useful technical evidence but are not Indonesian law.

### GATE-01 — Licensed standards and exact technical clauses

- **Blocks:** Exact SNI/ISO/ASTM requirements, definitions, equations, classifications, specimen preparation, tolerances, numerical limits, test conditions, and pass/fail language.
- **Resolution:** Obtain the controlling licensed full text and confirm edition/status with BSN or the standards body. Have the relevant competent professional identify the applicable clauses and permitted quotation.
- **Fallback:** State only the public catalog/abstract scope and omit the exact requirement.

### GATE-02 — Current jurisdiction and project applicability

- **Blocks:** Claims that a permit, approval, regulation, certificate class, maintenance duty, or inspection procedure applies to a particular Indonesian building, locality, employer, method, or date.
- **Resolution:** Check current national and local official sources, the building function/ownership, site rules, contract, and competent Indonesian legal/K3/building authority. Read Permenaker 9/2016 together with the partial changes in Permenaker 11/2026.
- **Fallback:** Provide a verification checklist and clearly label applicability unresolved.

### GATE-03 — Site-specific access, safety, equipment, anchor, and rescue evidence

- **Blocks:** Selecting or approving a MEWP, scaffold, gondola/BMU, rope-access system, anchor, fall-protection setup, weather limit, exclusion zone, or rescue method.
- **Resolution:** Obtain site survey, equipment/system identity and inspection records, support/anchor/design evidence, competent-person plan, risk assessment, method statement, team credentials, weather process, public controls, and rehearsable rescue arrangements.
- **Fallback:** Compare decision inputs only; do not prescribe or approve the method.

### GATE-04 — Exact glazing and façade condition

- **Blocks:** Identification of glass/heat treatment/lamination/coating surface, attribution of a scratch/leak/fogging/seal defect, restoration feasibility, and material compatibility.
- **Resolution:** Obtain glazing schedule, markings and product records, construction and coating location, edge/frame/seal/drainage condition, prior work, close inspection, and competent façade/glazing review where consequential.
- **Fallback:** Use observation-led triage and escalate; do not diagnose concealed construction or cause.

### GATE-05 — Cleaning product, tool, water, and adjacent-material compatibility

- **Blocks:** Exact chemical, dilution, dwell time, pH, scraper/pad/abrasive use, pressure, water-purity target, rinse method, or claim of harmlessness.
- **Resolution:** Obtain current product TDS/SDS, glass/coating/film/frame/seal/finish instructions, water data where relevant, a controlled test patch, runoff protection plan, and responsible approval.
- **Fallback:** Describe the least-aggressive verification sequence without product numbers or universal permission.

### GATE-06 — Exact film product, configuration, installation, care, and warranty

- **Blocks:** Film-to-glass approval, thermal compatibility, installation environment, seam/edge layout, cure duration, cleaning/removal method, defect tolerance, service life, and warranty.
- **Resolution:** Obtain the exact current manufacturer data sheet, film-to-glass table or written approval, installation/care instructions, product and glazing configuration, batch/installer authorization where required, warranty document, and project acceptance criteria.
- **Fallback:** Explain the screening workflow and label all product outcomes unresolved.

### GATE-07 — Performance, certification, test report, and building outcome

- **Blocks:** Numeric optical/solar/thermal/UV/acoustic/structural results, certification status, energy savings, temperature/comfort effects, glass capacity, or any “tested/passed/compliant” claim.
- **Resolution:** Obtain the current method and report/certification entry for the exact specimen/configuration; match dimensions, layers, attachment, frame/support, edition, laboratory, result, and limitations. Use project modeling or measurement for building outcomes.
- **Fallback:** Explain the metric or evidence hierarchy without assigning a value or outcome.

### GATE-08 — Safety/security threat and complete-system design

- **Blocks:** Claims about impact safety, forced entry, burglary delay, blast hazard, ballistics, life safety, or security adequacy.
- **Resolution:** Define the credible threat with a qualified security professional; obtain the complete tested system report and match glass, film, attachment, frame, supports, dimensions, installation, condition, and standard edition to the project.
- **Fallback:** State that film may be one component of a designed system and omit protection claims.

### GATE-09 — Contract, commercial, and operational facts

- **Blocks:** Live prices, service availability, schedule, insurance, scope inclusion, warranty remedy, liability, hidden-condition rights, change-order entitlement, and building/tenant approval.
- **Resolution:** Obtain dated survey, RFQ/quotation, contract, policy/certificate verification, warranty, building rules, named approvals, and professional advice where contractual or legal.
- **Fallback:** Discuss cost drivers and document fields only.

### GATE-10 — Competent façade, glazing, structural, or forensic review

- **Blocks:** Consequential decisions about cracks/chips, thermal stress, structural glass/load, anchors, spontaneous breakage, sealant design/repair, persistent leakage, IGU failure, or whether glass should remain in service.
- **Resolution:** Refer the exact site evidence and controlling documents to the appropriate competent façade/glazing/structural professional; commission testing or calculations where they specify.
- **Fallback:** Stop work around the affected area where safety could be involved and publish only escalation guidance.
