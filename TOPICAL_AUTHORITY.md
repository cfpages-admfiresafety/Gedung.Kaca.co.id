# Topical Authority — gedung.kaca.co.id

## Role and boundary

`gedung.kaca.co.id` is a specialist knowledge and commercial-support property for cleaning and maintaining building glass, plus installing and caring for architectural glass film or stickers. The two service intents stay distinct: `/cleaning-kaca/` owns the cleaning-service intent, while `/kaca-film/` owns the film-installation intent. Educational pages explain diagnosis, selection, planning, safety, quality control, and ownership decisions; they do not make unverified engineering, product-performance, certification, legal-compliance, warranty, or price claims.

Access-at-height methods, fall protection, rescue arrangements, glass and coating compatibility, cleaning chemistry, stain or biological-growth diagnosis, façade seals, film performance, thermal stress, weather limits, permits, standards, worker competence, warranty, and pricing require current primary evidence and site-specific professional review. Content must distinguish manufacturer data, official requirements, field observations, and editorial guidance. It must never present an article as a substitute for a competent façade consultant, occupational-safety professional, rope-access supervisor, glass or film manufacturer, or building manager.

## Internal-link rule

Each article links to its parent-topic hub and to the nearest next decision, not to a copied generic list. Diagnosis pages connect to compatibility tests and the appropriate cleaning or escalation route. Cleaning-method pages connect to access selection, safety controls, façade risks, and maintenance scheduling. Film-selection pages connect to performance interpretation, compatibility screening, installation planning, and lifecycle care. Procurement and handover pages link contextually to `/cleaning-kaca/` or `/kaca-film/` only after the reader has defined the relevant service scope.
